One app for local and long-distance transit across Europe. In Vienna you need one app, in Munich another, and a third for the train to Venice. Publit shows departures and connections wherever open timetables exist and guides you until you get off. No ads, no account, no tracking.

• Departures nearby: line, direction, platform and live times around you
• All connections on one timeline: see where you change and where it gets tight
• Smart routing: Publit rates every change. The route that feels shortest comes first
• Every step of the route in the colours of the lines, with platforms, stops and delays
• Live trip: your line, the minutes to your destination and where to get off, also on the lock screen
• New connection from here: missed a change? One tap finds the way from where you are
• Get-off reminder shortly before your stop
• Map with stations, lines, departures and exits; cafés, pharmacies and supermarkets nearby
• Tickets in one place: from PDF, photo or screenshot, read on your phone, nothing is uploaded
• Ticket links for rail and long-distance buses with start, destination and date filled in
• Wear OS: departures nearby and your trip under way on your wrist
• Widget with the next departures, calendar and sharing
• Light and dark, 15 languages

Timetables come from the open Transitous service (GTFS and GTFS-Realtime feeds of transport operators), maps from OpenFreeMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The coverage map on publit.eu shows how well your city is covered.

Publit is a community project from Vienna: suggest and vote on features at publit.eu/wuensche. All features stay free; if you like, you can support development voluntarily.
