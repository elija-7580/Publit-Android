Public transport, without the noise. Publit is a free, open-source transit app built on the open Transitous routing service. No ads, no account, no tracking.

• Departures nearby: line, direction, platform and live times around you
• All routes on one timeline: compare connections, see where you change and where it gets tight
• Every step of the route: map and steps in the colours of the lines, with platforms, stops and delays
• Smart routing: Publit rates how safely a change works and how long you wait and walk. The route that feels shortest comes first
• Live trip: your line, the minutes to your destination and where to get off, also as an ongoing notification
• New connection from here: missed a change? One tap finds the best way from where you are
• Get-off reminder shortly before your stop, with an announcement if you like
• Map with all stations, lines and departures right at the station
• One search for places, addresses and stops; links from Google Maps land in the right spot
• Cafés, pharmacies, supermarkets nearby on the map (OpenStreetMap)
• Tickets in the app: from PDF, photo or screenshot, read on your phone, nothing is uploaded
• Home, work, saved trips: stored only on your device
• Calendar and sharing, with the arrival time for the others
• Home screen widget with the next departures at a stop of your choice
• Light and dark, designed for day and night
• 15 languages

Data: routing and departures from Transitous (MOTIS, GTFS and GTFS-RT feeds of transport operators), maps from OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
